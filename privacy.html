Privacy Policy for Zone Vault

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

Welcome to Zone Vault. This page explains what information we collect when you sign up and watch streams on our site, why we need it, and how it is handled. We keep things simple and only collect what is needed to run the site and keep our streams private.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account and use Zone Vault, we store the following details:

  • Account Details: Your email address, login credentials (or Google Sign-In token), display name/username, profile avatar, and your Telegram handle (@username).
  • Watch History & Playback Progress: When you watch VODs or concert replays, we save the episode ID and your last playback timestamp so you can pick up right where you left off.
  • Live Chat & Community Posts: If you join a live stream chat or submit a message on community pages (like birthday boards), your username and message are visible to other logged-in members.
  • Stream Session Tokens: When you play a video, our server generates a temporary stream token tied to your account ID, username, and IP address to verify that you are an approved member watching directly on zonevault.live.

2. How We Use Your Information

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Everything we collect is used strictly to run Zone Vault:

  • Account Approval & Access Control: To check your membership status, process your join request, and make sure only approved accounts can open video pages and live broadcasts.
  • Stream & Content Protection: To block external downloaders, browser scraping extensions, unauthorized hotlinking, and screen-recording tools from ripping videos or subtitles.
  • Saved Progress & Preferences: We use your browser's local storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) to remember your dark/light theme setting and keep your session active when you refresh a page or switch episodes.
  • Admin Updates: Your Telegram username is used by admins to reach out if there is an issue with your access request or account.

3. Data Storage & Security

Account profiles, watch history, and live chat data are stored through Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database).

  • Passwords: Your password is handled directly by Google Firebase Authentication. Zone Vault admins cannot view or recover your raw password.
  • Encrypted Stream Delivery: Video manifests and subtitle files are loaded in memory inside the player and are locked to active member sessions.
  • Account Isolation: Your private profile details and watch history are restricted to your account. Other regular members cannot view your watch history or email address.

4. Third-Party Services

We rely on Google Firebase for sign-in and database storage, and Cloudflare to deliver pages and protect streams from automated scraping. If you choose to sign in with Google, Google shares your basic account identifier and email with our login system under their standard privacy terms.

5. Contact & Account Deletion

If you have any questions about this policy or want to have your account and watch history deleted, message our admin directly on Telegram at @Hoskwi_17.

By registering and using Zone Vault, you agree to the practices described on this page.
