Player Controls Guide

PC / Laptop Controls:
← Left Arrow: Rewind 10 seconds
→ Right Arrow: Fast-forward 10 seconds
↑ Up Arrow: Increase volume
↓ Down Arrow: Decrease volume
F / f: Fullscreen
M / m: Mute / Unmute

Cellphone Controls:
Touch the screen: Show / hide controls
Double-tap center: Pause / play
Double-tap left: Rewind 10 seconds
Double-tap right: Fast-forward 10 seconds

Important Reminder:
Please note that these controls may not work on all mobile devices due to differences in device compatibility and browser settings. If you experience any issues with the player or its controls, please contact the admin on Telegram at @Hoskwi_17 for assistance.
